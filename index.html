Add Tip Tracker to your Home Screen

Get one-tap access to Tip Tracker and use it more like an app.

iPhone / iPad

1. Open Tip Tracker in Safari.

2. Tap the Share button (the square with the ↑ arrow).

3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

4. Tap Add.

Android

1. Open Tip Tracker in Chrome.

2. Tap the ⋮ menu.

3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

4. Tap Add or Install.
